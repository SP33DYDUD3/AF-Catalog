How to Order

Welcome, to our catalog, hope you find what you're looking for. A few things to know before you reach out.

Time to Order

Please order at least 3 days in advance. Significantly larger arrangements may need 4 - 5 days. We do take same-day requests, it just depends on floral availability that we have at the time.

The process

  1. Browse and reach out with the name and date you need it for.
  2. We'll confirm the details with you and provide a final price.
  3. Payment is processed and the order is confirmed.

Payment & Deposit

We accept payment over the phone or via Zelle. You're also welcome to stop by in person before the order is due. A non-refundable 25% deposit of the arrangement's price is required to confirm your order.

Delivery

Delivery is $15 within Richmond and San Pablo. For neighboring cities, please check with us on availability and pricing.
